IDRIVE SUPPORT GUIDES WEBSITE

Files:
- index.html: homepage
- blog.html: searchable directory of all 100 articles
- support.html: support preparation and safety guidance
- articles/: 100 individual HTML articles
- sitemap.xml: sitemap containing homepage, directory, support page and all article URLs
- robots.txt: crawler instructions

Publishing:
1. Upload the contents of this folder to your website hosting root.
2. Replace https://your-idrive-domain.example in sitemap.xml and robots.txt with your actual published domain.
3. Keep the articles folder and its relative paths intact.

Buttons:
The IDrive Login and IDrive Support buttons appear only in the top navigation bar. They are 3D-styled on-page buttons with no external URL or redirect; clicking them reveals a short instruction panel. Article pages use parent-relative navigation links so Home, Articles and Support work from the articles folder.

Content:
Each article is written as an independent educational guide. Verify product-specific steps against current IDrive documentation before relying on them. Never share account passwords or verification codes.
